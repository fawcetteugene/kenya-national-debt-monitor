Debt servicing
Interest eats revenue
A large share of Kenya's annual budget goes to servicing existing debt, limiting investment in schools, hospitals, and infrastructure.
Currency risk
KSh depreciation amplifies
Most external debt is denominated in USD or EUR. When the shilling weakens, the KSh value of debt rises automatically, creating pressure even without new borrowing.
Action needed
What must happen
Revenue collection improvement, spending efficiency, and export-led GDP growth are the fastest paths to reducing the debt-to-GDP ratio sustainably.

Source warning
    Important note

    This counter is a simulation. The base figure is the official World Bank external debt stock for Kenya. The live counter then applies a 12% annual growth rate (compounded continuously) from the moment the data was loaded, updating every 100 milliseconds. Exchange rates use a live API. This is not an official government or IMF figure — it is an educational tool to illustrate debt dynamics.
